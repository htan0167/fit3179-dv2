01

THE SCALE

A larger workforce.
A similar share of the economy.

Employment grew 33.0% from 2008–09 to 2023–24. Its share of all jobs barely changed: 4.0% to 4.1%. The wider labour market grew too.

02

THE PEOPLE

Creative careers extend
beyond creative businesses.

Creative employment depends on both the job and the industry. BCARR identifies three groups:

42.7% · SPECIALISED

Creative role.
Creative industry.

For example, a performer working in a performing arts organisation.

39.1% · EMBEDDED

Creative role.
Another industry.

For example, a graphic designer working in a healthcare organisation.

18.2% · SUPPORT

Another role.
Creative industry.

For example, an accountant working in a creative business.

Examples illustrate the official definitions; they are not separate observations in the dataset.

03

THE PLACES

The east holds the largest hubs.
Growth tells a different story.

NSW and Victoria hold 65.6% of the workforce in 2023–24. Victoria grew fastest since 2008–09; NT and ACT finished below their starting estimates.

04

THE WIDER CONNECTION

Culture reaches further
than paid employment.

32.2% of adults participated in a cultural activity in 2021–22. Participation and attendance reveal another side of cultural life.

A different measure. These ABS charts cover participation and attendance among people aged 15+. They measure cultural engagement, not jobs; comparisons do not establish causation.

BEYOND THE SPOTLIGHT

Culture connects workers, businesses and communities. Employment has grown and reaches beyond creative industries. Participation adds another perspective on cultural life.

Data preparation, geography & limitations

The four CSVs were transcribed from numerical tables in official publications and reformatted for these charts. Growth, contribution, indices and shares are calculated from those estimates; no additional employment observations were invented. Displayed percentages may differ slightly because of rounding. Workforce and participation datasets are shown as complementary views; they have not been merged into a causal analysis.

Geographic boundaries: Natural Earth, 1:50m Admin 1 states and provinces, public domain. Eight Australian jurisdictions were retained and exported as a quantised TopoJSON for fast loading. Boundary data source ↗. Small territories not represented in the statistical tables are omitted. All charts and diagrams were constructed specifically for this project.
